# Phase 3F Exchangeability Result

The five UPV development sessions do not support the frozen cross-session stochastic-generalization claim. Three of five real held-out sessions were jointly and temporally compatible with their real training-session envelope; four were required.

The failures are not uniform. Corrected Test 2 fails the real-to-real joint-distribution comparison, while corrected Test 5 fails the temporal comparison. A holdout-fitted 60-second-block diagnostic oracle also passes only the other three folds. The oracle was diagnostic and cannot be selected.

This means that further tuning of one global HMM or VAR is not the justified next step. The available data support a narrower direct measured-trace emulator: select a development trace for a declared scenario, transform its relative RSRP and SINR into bounded RFsim controls, and assess whether RFsim reproduces that trace. Such an experiment can support trace-level emulation, not cross-scenario or population generalization.

Corrected Test 1 ASUS is the appropriate source because it was designated as the clean single-active-UE survey before this diagnostic. Test 6 remains unopened. No reservation is requested until the direct-trace control envelope, safety states, and rollback rules are frozen.
